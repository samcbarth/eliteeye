Why this exists

The gift is never really about the gift

It is about being seen. A gift that lands says I paid attention. A gift that misses says the opposite, loudly, and it is remembered.

I know how frustrating it is to tell someone exactly what you want and watch them miss the mark. And for the rest of us — we genuinely want to give something beautiful, but it is time-consuming, and the good things are never on the first page of results.

Elite Eye takes the searching off your plate. I find the pieces you would not otherwise find, and I present them so the moment of opening is as considered as the object itself.

Questions

Before you book

What does the consultation cost?

The introductory call is complimentary. If we move forward, engagements are quoted based on scope; bespoke work generally begins at $800 plus the cost of the pieces themselves.

Do you work outside Dallas?

Yes. Most clients are in Highland Park, University Park, Preston Hollow, and the surrounding area, but sourcing and delivery are not geographically limited.

How do you handle authenticity?

Every designer item is either sourced through an authorized channel or put through an authentication process before it reaches you. This is non-negotiable.

How far ahead should I book?

For the holidays, by early autumn — the best pieces are limited and they go. For an individual occasion, two to three weeks is comfortable. Shorter timelines are sometimes possible; ask.

Can you work within a set budget?

Yes, and I prefer to. Tell me the number on the first call and every option you see will respect it.

Is the packaging discreet?

Yes. The presentation inside is styled to be photographed; the shipping packaging outside gives nothing away.

Give something they could not have found themselves

A short call is the whole commitment. Tell me who it is for and I will tell you what is possible.
