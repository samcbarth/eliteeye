Corporate & Executive Gifting

A gifting program for the clients and partners you cannot afford to send a branded tumbler.

Book a complimentary consultation

At a certain level, a corporate gift is a positioning statement. It tells your best client what tier of relationship they are in — and a generic one tells them something you did not intend.

I build gifting programs for firms, family offices, and private practices: a defined tier structure, a sourcing calendar ahead of the holiday crush, and a record of who received what, so no one is sent the same thing twice.

Nothing is logo-stamped unless you ask. The point is that it does not look like it came from a catalog.
