Weddings & Milestones

Bridal party gifts, anniversary pieces, and milestone gifting handled as one cohesive collection rather than nine separate errands.

Book a complimentary consultation

A wedding produces a dozen gifting decisions at exactly the moment you have the least attention to spend on them: the bridal party, the parents, the host, the planner, the couple themselves.

I treat them as one collection. Consistent in taste, individual to each person, and delivered on a schedule that does not depend on you remembering it.

The same applies to the milestones that follow — anniversaries, births, the birthdays that carry weight.
